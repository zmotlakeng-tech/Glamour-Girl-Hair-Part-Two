Our Story

Glamour Girl Hair was founded in 2022 with one goal: to bring premium Vietnamese straight, super double-drawn hair bundles to South African women who want a luxury look without the luxury markup.

Our Mission Vision

Our vision is to expand globally and become the most trusted and glamorous hair brand on the market. Our mission is to empower women to express their beauty, confidence and individuality through premium-quality human hair.

What Makes Us Different

  • Genuine Vietnamese straight, super double-drawn bundles never chemically treated
  • Hair that lasts two to three years with proper care
  • Real reviews and before-and-after photos, not just stock imagery
  • Direct, personal service via WhatsApp
